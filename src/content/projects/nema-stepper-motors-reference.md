# **NEMA Stepper Motors Reference**

NiceGUI reference app for NEMA stepper motor frame sizes (NEMA 8 – NEMA 42).

Shows typical flange size, mounting hole spacing, shaft diameter/type, body length range, holding torque range and phase current for each frame. Values are illustrative — always check the datasheet of the specific motor.
